Student workspace

Students can complete profiles, upload clinical histories, see their locations and conditions, track submission status, read feedback, and review results.

  • History upload and document review
  • Submission status and feedback
  • Results, profile, locations, and conditions
02

Clinical staff workspace

Clinical staff can create structured evaluations, select students and sites, capture scores, add feedback, and keep the complete student context attached.

  • Assessment creation and editing
  • Structured clinical evaluation fields
  • Examiner feedback and signatures
03

Institution workspace

Administrators and academic leads can manage people, sites, curriculum, progression, oversight, moderation, and reporting from one place.

  • Users, staff, students, and clinical sites
  • Analytics, audit logs, and reporting
  • Duplicate, plagiarism, and FQE moderation

Assessment coverage

Built around the way clinical education is actually assessed.

The application includes dedicated flows for several structured assessment formats, so teams can standardise the process without flattening the clinical context.

A

Pre-FQE assessments

Prepare students for high-stakes evaluation with structured pre-FQE workflows.

F

FQE long case

Capture long-case assessment details, examiners, feedback, and review states.

H

HSM oral

Support oral assessment workflows with the relevant clinical context and examiner actions.

C

Community health

Manage community health assessments alongside sites, histories, and student records.

S

Short case

Use a focused workflow for short-case evaluations and structured scoring.

R

Review history

Keep a longitudinal view of assessments, results, feedback, and previous submissions.
