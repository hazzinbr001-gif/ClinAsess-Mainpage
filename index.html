Academic integrity

Identify potential duplication before grading.

ClinAssess compares submissions within an assessment record and highlights potential matches for review.

  • Every submission is compared within the institution's assessment record.
  • Potential matches are sent to an examiner for review. A flag is not a verdict.
  • Institutions keep a clear record of decisions and assessment progress.

Examiners review the evidence and make the final decision.

Reviewers see the flagged submissions side by side and record their decision in the platform.
